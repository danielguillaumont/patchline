# Methodology / draft

- KEV catalog inclusion is not proof of exposure for a particular organization.
- KEV `dateAdded` refers to catalog addition, not exploitation start.
- EPSS predicts exploitation activity over a 30-day horizon; percentile is a relative ranking.
- The P1 fixture is synthetic and is not real 2026 source data.
- Source downloads, provenance, snapshot dates and quality gates are scheduled for P2–P3.
